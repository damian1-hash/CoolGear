Hygiene standard

Worried it isn't clean?

Fair question. We only accept materials that can be fully sanitised between people, like medical-grade silicone, glass and steel. Before you pay, you see a live photo of the exact item.

Accepted
  • Medical-grade silicone
  • Glass and stainless steel
  • Hard plastic (ABS), ceramic, aluminium
  • Vibrators with a removable silicone sleeve
Never accepted
  • Jelly, TPE and TPR
  • Latex and realistic-skin toys
  • Sealed vibrators coated in TPE

These materials hold bacteria even after cleaning, so they can't be shared safely.

Who does the cleaning?

The owner, following the CoolGear cleaning checklist before every rental, and removable sleeves are replaced or sanitised. We can't be in every owner's home, so we also suggest giving it a quick clean yourself. With non-porous materials, that's simple and reliable.

Leather and fabric gear, such as harnesses and cuffs, will be added once our wipe-down standard has been tested in Oslo.

Questions

Asked privately. Answered plainly.

Someone else has used this. Is it actually clean?

Some materials, such as jelly, TPE and latex, can't be made safe to share, so we don't allow them. Everything on CoolGear is non-porous, like medical-grade silicone, glass or steel, and can be fully sanitised. Owners follow a cleaning checklist before every rental, and you see a live photo of the exact item before you pay. Many people also give it their own clean when it arrives, and we'd encourage that.

Will anyone find out I used this?

The other person only ever sees your username. Your real name and address are never shared, and emails from us never mention what you rented. Your bank statement will show [billing name]. Your ID is checked once, stored securely, and kept no longer than needed.

Is it safe to meet a stranger?

Every member has passed an ID check and a live selfie, so you're meeting a verified person, not an anonymous account. Meet-ups happen in public places you both agree on, and everything is paid beforehand, so no money changes hands in person. If something doesn't feel right, don't go ahead, and tell us.

What if the item isn't as described, or I don't get my deposit back?

You approve a live photo with a one-time code word before you pay, so the listing can't use an old or borrowed picture. Your deposit is held by our payment provider, not by the owner, and comes back automatically once you both confirm the return. If there's a disagreement, the photos taken at handoff and return decide it.

What if I lend my gear and never see it again?

Every renter is ID-verified with a card on file. If an item isn't back within 72 hours, the full deposit goes to you. If it's never returned, the renter is banned and charged its value. We can't promise nothing will ever go wrong, but we can make sure there are real consequences when it does.

What happens if I return something late?

You'll get a reminder, and a small daily late fee starts. After 72 hours, the full deposit goes to the owner and the account is suspended until it's sorted out.

Can it be delivered instead?

Not at launch. In Oslo, you meet in a public place you both agree on. Courier delivery in plain, unmarked packaging is planned for later.

Can I pay in cash or Vipps at the meet-up?

No. Every payment goes through CoolGear before the handoff. That's what makes the deposit, the refund and our help with disagreements work. Deals made outside CoolGear have no protection.

Who can join?

Any verified adult, 18 or older. CoolGear is built with the gay community in mind and is open to everyone.
